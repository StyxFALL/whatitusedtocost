Privacy Policy — YT What Channel

What It Used To Cost · last updated 30 September 2026

This policy covers the private software used to publish the YouTube channel What It Used To Cost. The software is operated by the channel owner and is not offered to other people.

Who this applies to

One person: the channel owner. The application has no other users, no sign-up, and no accounts. It is not a public service.

What the application accesses

With the channel owner's authorization through Google, the application uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload video files to the owner's own channel
  • set the title, description, tags, category and thumbnail of those videos
  • read the channel's own name and identifier, to confirm it is uploading to the right channel

It does not read, collect or process the data of any other person, channel or viewer.

What is stored, and where

  • A Google OAuth refresh token, stored only on the owner's own computer, used to renew access for uploading.
  • Video files, thumbnails and the text of episode descriptions, on the same computer.

Nothing is stored on a server operated by the channel owner, because there is none.

What is not done

  • No personal data is collected from viewers or from anyone else.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking code runs in this application.
  • No data is used to train machine-learning models.

Third parties involved

Publishing a video necessarily sends it to Google (YouTube). Public statistical data is fetched from the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, FRED, the U.S. Energy Information Administration, Eurostat, the International Labour Organization, the World Bank, the International Monetary Fund and the Bank for International Settlements. Those requests contain no personal information.

Withdrawing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately stops the application from reaching the channel.

Retention

The refresh token is kept until access is revoked or it is deleted from the computer. Video files are kept as long as they are useful for production.
